# What this package preserves

This is a portable revision of an existing project-level `engineering-delivery-team` skill, not a claim that a new team was invented from scratch. Its role contracts, exclusive file ownership, independent verification, and operational review come from that earlier package.

The following are generalized lessons from prototype conversations and review work. Conversation reports of past tests or deployment are historical reports, not fresh verification of those applications.

| Experience | Reusable lesson | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regional trip planner | A working market guide is a useful slice but does not deliver the original day-planning outcome. Preserve the wider journey and explicitly name the remaining gap. | Product review, acceptance contract |
| Regional trip planner | Operator claims can conflict; old sources can reveal candidates without proving present availability. Community feedback is evidence for moderation, not automatic canonical truth. | Data/evidence role, product guidance |
| Regional trip planner | A map and list must share selection and filters. Test the full experience without requiring map interaction. | Design and journey QA |
| Regional trip planner | A visual app builder may not support the requested backend. Keep the user's goal, disclose platform limits, and preserve an honest integration boundary. | Discovery and handoff guidance |
| Community-platform prototype | Functional parity needs an inventory of existing roles and flows. Identity selectors, overflow, operator onboarding, configuration, and click attribution are product work, not finishing decoration. | Product guidance, visual and operational gates |
| Local recording prototype | One implementation owner with bounded read-only review can reveal regressions without reviewers silently rewriting code. Recheck the exact failed cases after corrections. | Role briefs, verification guidance |
| Local recording prototype | Background work, capture, saved user choices, and generated output have different lifecycles. Verify retry, restart, identity, partial failure, and truthful readiness. | Verification guidance |
| Local recording prototype | Model repair must preserve supported work, expose uncertainty, and avoid equating successful execution with dependable output. | AI and provenance guidance |

Project-specific markets, political decisions, data, business rules, local paths, chat IDs, private memories, test recordings, and service configurations are excluded. These examples guide the method; they do not silently impose one project's choices on another.
