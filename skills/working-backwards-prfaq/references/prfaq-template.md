## Future press release

### Headline

**[Organization or product] introduces [solution] so [specific customer] can [meaningful outcome].**

### Subheadline

Add one sentence that makes the customer, experience, or differentiation more concrete.

### Dateline and introduction

From a believable future date, explain what launched, for whom, what changed, and why it matters now.

### Customer problem

Describe the current job, friction, workaround, and consequence in the customer's language. Avoid a generic market overview.

### Solution and experience

Explain the beginning-to-end experience and why it resolves the stated problem. Focus on outcomes; include only the features needed to make the experience credible.

### Illustrative leader quote

Explain why the organization chose this problem and the principle behind the solution. Label the quote as illustrative in drafts.

### How the customer uses it

Describe discovery, onboarding, first value, repeat use, failure or recovery, and the next step.

### Illustrative customer quote

Express a believable before-and-after outcome without inventing a real endorsement.

### Availability

State the initial population, eligibility, rollout boundary, and customer call to action.

## Internal FAQ

### Decision and strategy

1. What decision does this document request?
2. Why this problem, customer, and moment?
3. How does the direction support the product or company strategy?
4. What is the strongest alternative, including doing nothing?
5. What evidence would change the recommendation?

### Customer and problem

6. Who are the user, buyer, beneficiary, and affected non-user?
7. What job are they trying to accomplish today?
8. What evidence demonstrates frequency, severity, and willingness to change?
9. What might disappoint, harm, confuse, or exclude the customer?

### Product and differentiation

10. What behavior are we trying to create?
11. What is the minimum lovable or learnable scope?
12. What is explicitly out of scope and why can it wait?
13. Why will customers switch from the status quo or alternatives?
14. What feedback loop or incentive changes, including second-order effects?

### Measurement and economics

15. What is the primary outcome metric and formula?
16. What are the population, baseline source, target, time window, and decision rule?
17. What guardrails could invalidate a nominal win?
18. What are the unit economics, cost drivers, and operational burden?

### Delivery, trust, and operations

19. What are the largest value, usability, feasibility, viability, security, privacy, legal, and operational risks?
20. Which teams, systems, policies, or partners are dependencies?
21. What is the rollout, support, rollback, and incident model?
22. What is the most contentious decision and strongest counterargument?

### AI / agent contract, if applicable

23. What is the agent's bounded job and authoritative state?
24. Which tools and actions are automatic, confirmed, approved, escalated, or prohibited?
25. What are the quality floor, target, delight level, evaluation set, and silent failure signals?
26. What are the cost, latency, quality, recovery, auditability, and human-override requirements?
