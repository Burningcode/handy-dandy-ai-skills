# Product Vision Alignment Canvas

This is an original public template for the `product-vision-alignment` skill.

```markdown
# Product Vision Alignment Canvas — [product or portfolio]

| Vision owner | Horizon | Audience | Decision enabled | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Name or role] | [Time horizon] | [Who must understand or align] | [Next choice] | [H / M / L + why] |

## User transformation

| Focus user | Current struggle | Future capability or behavior | Meaningful outcome | Outside initial focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Specific user] | [Evidence-backed reality] | [Solution-independent change] | [Why it matters] | [Boundary] |

## Why this future

| Change making it possible | Differentiated advantage | User proof needed | Strategic relevance | Evidence / assumption |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Market, behavior, or technology shift] | [Why this organization can matter] | [What users must validate] | [Business connection] | [Label + source] |

## Future experience arc

| Moment | User context | New behavior or capability | Value realized | Risk or friction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Trigger / progress / outcome] | [Situation] | [Change] | [Benefit] | [Cost or harm] |

## Boundaries and tensions

| We intend | We do not promise | Tension to resolve | Evidence that could change the vision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Durable intent] | [Explicit non-promise] | [Trade-off] | [Signal] |

## Alignment playback

- **Participants:** [Users, builders, operators, decision-makers]
- **Story to tell:** [Current reality -> future experience -> value]
- **Decisions required:** [Named choices and owners]
- **Questions that invite dissent:** [What feels wrong, missing, harmful, or implausible?]
- **Evidence gaps:** [Unknowns and owner]
- **Next playback / revision trigger:** [Date or condition]
```

For a storyboard, convert the future-experience rows into a short sequence of moments. Preserve the user context, behavior, value, and friction in every frame; do not turn it into a feature tour.
