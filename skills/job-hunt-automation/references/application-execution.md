# Authorized application execution

## Before transmitting data

Confirm the named employer, exact title/requisition, official application destination, scope of approval, prepared packet, and candidate profile version. A candidate-approved named batch covers ordinary form completion, attachment upload, correction, submission, and reconciliation only for those roles, subject to current tool/browser confirmation rules. A newly discovered role is not automatically in that batch. Recheck the official posting and tracker for closure, duplication, location/compensation conflicts, or a changed form target.

## Form completion

- Review role-tailored resume and letter against the verified baseline. Check file type, filename or hash, and page/render quality when documents are generated. Upload the resume to the resume field and a letter only to its distinct field or as requested. Verify the attached filenames after upload; a text fallback is not the same as a transmitted DOCX.
- Map fields by meaning, not by positional order. Distinguish current employer from most recent employer, legal from preferred name, phone country code from phone number, and question-level SMS from account-level SMS. Reinspect values after autofill and validation errors.
- Answer from verified evidence and candidate-approved preferences. Keep optional demographics, privacy acknowledgment, talent-pool retention, AI use, marketing, and recruiting SMS as separate choices. Do not interpret consent to one as consent to another.
- Stop on an unsupported legal/history/conflict declaration, sensitive disclosure outside the approved scope, misleading bundled option, materially changed role, or role-specific mismatch. Ask the candidate for the exact missing decision, and continue independent roles in the authorized batch.
- Do not generate a first-person answer when the employer forbids AI assistance or requires a personally authored response. The candidate handles assessments, signatures, passwords, MFA, emailed codes, and CAPTCHAs. Ordinary account-profile fields may be staged within the approved scope, but never choose credentials or bypass authentication for the candidate.

## Review, submit, verify

At final review, inspect role/requisition; identity and contact; location and eligibility; work authorization/sponsorship; resume and letter; experience and education; salary/availability; referral; all employer-specific questions; and each separate consent setting. Fix visible errors and check that changes persisted. Submit only within the active authorization and the environment's action-time controls.

Prefer an employer confirmation page, application-status record, or receipt tied to the exact requisition. If the result is ambiguous, mark `Submission uncertain`, inspect candidate home/email if authorized, and do not click Submit again until duplicate risk is resolved. If the candidate performed a human-only final step and reports completion, label it candidate-confirmed rather than employer-confirmed unless an employer state is visible.

Fresh-read the tracker, add or update only the exact role after confirmation, preserve existing validation and formatting, and read back the written record. Store the evidence type, date, transmitted-artifact state, and next follow-up. Report unfinished forms with their exact final verified state and candidate action rather than claiming that the whole batch was submitted.
