# Candidate setup interview

Use this as a progressive interview, not a form dumped on the candidate. Ask only questions needed for the next requested mode. Offer `unknown`, `skip`, and `decide per application` where appropriate. Record each answer with date, source, scope, and whether the candidate verified it. Do not infer protected traits or legal answers from a resume or location.

## 1. Goal and operating state

- Are you exploring, actively applying, interviewing, or paused after an offer? What outcome and time horizon matter now?
- What role families, levels, functions, industries, company stages, and growth trajectory are in and out? Which are preferences versus hard exclusions?
- Is the priority quality, speed, volume, compensation, learning, stability, mission, or another tradeoff? What would make an exceptional stretch worth considering?
- Should the system discover only, prepare packets, fill forms for review, or submit within a named approved batch? Who may approve, and how long does an approval remain valid?

## 2. Location, work, and compensation

- What is your home work location (city/state/country), remote eligibility, commute radius, relocation stance, travel limit, and required hours or time zone?
- What pay measure matters: base, total compensation, OTE, contract rate, or another figure? What are target and hard floor, currency, and whether an undisclosed range may proceed to review?
- Are there role-specific exceptions, and do they authorize review only or application too?

## 3. Verified candidate facts and materials

- Which resume or career record is canonical? Which dates, titles, employers, achievements, metrics, education, certifications, portfolio links, and work samples are verified? Which claims require qualification?
- What name and contact details should applications use? Where may private answers and artifacts be stored? Do not copy sensitive values into this skill package.
- Do you have truthful, reusable answers for employment status, notice period, compensation expectations, work authorization, sponsorship, location, and availability? Which must be reconfirmed per employer?
- What examples can you personally explain in an interview? Is there an answer bank of candidate-authored stories, and what is its source and update date?

## 4. Eligibility and declarations

Ask when relevant to a role, never as a blanket inference: authorization and sponsorship; citizenship or clearance requirements; noncompete or contractual obligations; previous employment or interview at the employer; related-party, referral, government, background-check, or conflict disclosures. Preserve the employer's exact question and option wording. An unanswered declaration blocks that field rather than becoming an assumed `No`.

## 5. Consent and privacy

- For optional demographic, disability, veteran, gender, pronoun, or orientation fields, should the default be omit/decline, use separately recorded answers, or ask each time? Record only answers the candidate voluntarily provides, with purpose and disclosure scope.
- May the employer use candidate data for recruiting? Distinguish required applicant privacy acknowledgment from optional talent-pool retention, AI matching/review, marketing, and SMS. Ask separately when options are bundled or consequential.
- Are AI-assisted drafts acceptable to the candidate? Employer restrictions override this preference. Candidate-original fields must be written by the candidate.
- What data may be sent to connected tools or cloud services? Where are private files stored, and what should be deleted or retained after the search?

## 6. Access and workflow

- What is the canonical tracker, and which tabs/statuses are authoritative? What counts as a verified submission? What follow-up interval is wanted?
- Which job sources and signed-in browser accounts may be read? Is email in scope? Are outreach and LinkedIn actions in scope, separately from applying?
- Who handles passwords, MFA, emailed codes, CAPTCHAs, signatures, assessments, and employer account creation? Default: the candidate.
- For a blocked form, should it remain as a tab, be saved as a draft, or be handed off as a link plus exact next step? Which of those states actually persists across browser restarts?
- Should scheduled runs be created? If yes, agree on cadence, notification threshold, pause behavior, and whether they stage only. A schedule does not inherit authority for future submissions.

## Finish setup

Summarize the candidate's hard gates, soft preferences, confirmed facts, consent defaults, authority boundary, storage locations, tracker, and unresolved questions. Ask the candidate to correct that summary before the first submission batch. Keep the filled profile private and version it when answers change; do not silently convert one-off role exceptions into global preferences.
