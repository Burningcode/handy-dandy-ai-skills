## Visual rendering contract

Render journey phases left to right at wide widths and stack them in sequence on narrow screens. Keep the as-is and to-be states aligned by phase so the change is directly comparable. Show evidence confidence in text. Place release slices beneath the journey backbone and keep every slice end to end. The first view must be useful without interaction; expansion may reveal sources or story detail.

Do not use decorative empathy scores, invented emotional states, or a wall of disconnected sticky notes. The canvas exists to support a named product decision.
